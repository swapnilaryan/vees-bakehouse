How it’s made

Every layer, a little love.

Chocolate sponge, fresh from the oven

Every celebration starts here.

Layered with rich ganache

Generously, between every tier.

Wrapped in chocolate

Smoothed all round and dotted with gold.

A topper, rosettes and pearls

Finished by hand, just for your day.

Ready for the candles

And for everyone who gathers round it.

And the cookies

Little bites of happiness.

It starts with a dough ball

Rolled and ready on the tray.

Pressed and loaded

Chocolate chips, and then a few more.

Baked till golden

The smell that brings everyone to the kitchen.

Made for sharing

With chai, with friends, with the people you love.

Here’s to the moments
you’ll remember.

From first birthdays to fiftieth anniversaries, and every chai-time in between.
